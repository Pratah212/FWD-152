Laboratory of Social Analytics and Network Science (LSANS)

South Asian University

Welcome to LSANS!!

Our Laboratory focuses on advancing social analytics and network science, specifically focusing on the critical aspects of Fairness, Robustness, and Diversification within complex modern social and informational networks.

The overarching goal of any social or network algorithm is to extract meaningful insights or optimize dissemination while preserving computational performance and guaranteeing ethical constraints.
