Topics such as algorithm auditing, resilient system architecture, and bias detection in automated systems are all subjects of investigation.

Current work in the lab

  • Influence Maximization models that ensure equitable spread across different communities and demographics.
  • Link Prediction algorithms that account for inherent fairness constraints and minimize systemic biases.
  • Community Detection methods that are robust against adversarial attacks and network noise.
  • Information Dissemination analysis to understand and counteract the spread of misinformation or echo chambers.
  • Heuristic and Meta-heuristic Algorithms (evolutionary computing, swarm intelligence) for NP-hard problems like influence maximization in multi-million node networks.
  • Soft Computing Techniques (fuzzy logic, machine learning, AI) to model ambiguity, noise, and semantic uncertainty in social media data.
  • Advanced Graph Theory such as spectral graph theory, hypergraphs, and graph motifs to capture higher-order, multi-way interactions.
  • Quantum Computing and Quantum Walk Algorithms for quantum-enhanced community detection and graph isomorphism challenges.
  • Approximation algorithms that integrate societal considerations: algorithmic equity, transparency, and user privacy, while mitigating digital echo chambers and polarization.
